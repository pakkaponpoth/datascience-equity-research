⚡ The 60-second version
SETScout finds Thai stocks worth researching — and tells you the risk, honestly. It does not predict prices or tell you to buy. Every day it scores 95 SET100 stocks on 4 simple factors, sorts them into Worth a look 🟢 / Wait 🟡 / Not now 🔴, and explains each in plain sentences with the risk in baht. It even admits when it can't beat the market — that honesty is the point.

🔍 The detailed version

What it is (and isn't). SETScout is an Explainable Investment Decision Support System for the Thai market. It answers “which stocks should I spend time researching today, and how risky are they?” — not “what will the price be” and not “buy this.” Discovery + risk + explanation, with the disclaimer everywhere.

There used to be a fifth, Value (price vs its own 200-day average). We removed it on 9 Sep 2026 after measuring that it correlated −0.93 with Momentum — it was that factor with the sign flipped, not a separate one. Growth (the 12-month return) was replaced by ROE on 16 Sep 2026: it overlapped Momentum (r = +0.66), and ROE is the first factor read from company accounts. · เคยมีปัจจัยที่ 5 ชื่อ Value แต่ตัดออกแล้ว เพราะซ้ำกับโมเมนตัม · ปัจจัย Growth ถูกแทนด้วย ROE
Each factor is z-scored (put on a fair 0-centred scale) across all 95 stocks — the same group the rank "#7 / 95" compares against. Until 21 Sep 2026 scores were also adjusted within each sector; that step was dropped, so the calmest sector, banks, now fills much of the safer lists. · ให้คะแนนเทียบกับหุ้นทั้ง 95 ตัว (เลิกปรับตามกลุ่มอุตสาหกรรมแล้ว) หุ้นธนาคารซึ่งราคานิ่งจึงติดรายการสายปลอดภัยมาก

Measured 7 Nov 2026 by backtest_profiles.py with the AHP weights, 73 start-points; buy-and-hold here is an equal-weight basket of all 95, +8.6% a year.

Read it honestly: the defensive engines don't beat the market — that's expected (efficient markets) and it's the thesis, not a bug. On this window the aggressive engine beats buy-and-hold by 4.3 points and 100% of random portfolios — but this is the decade we had already examined about fifteen times, re-run after the model changed. The test we fixed in advance, on sealed 1999–2014 prices, reached only 81% (pass mark 90%) and won 2 of 5 blocks, so we do not call it an edge. Caveats we keep out loud: survivorship bias in old data, momentum can crash, one market/period.

The honesty rails (why judges trust it)
Luck bar — we compare against hundreds of random portfolios. Real skill beats most of them; luck doesn't.
Score tested — we checked whether a higher score predicts a better next month. It doesn't (information coefficient +0.013, statistically zero). So the app shows no hit rate at all — a per-stock forecast the tests don't support is not information. We rank research-worthiness, not direction.
No look-ahead — every backtest scores using only past data, then measures the future.
Educational, not advice — soft wording, disclaimers everywhere, non-commercial.

Under the hood (for builders)

The engine runs ahead of time and writes a file; the website just reads it — so users never wait, and the site could be built before the engine even existed. Everything is Python + vanilla JS, no framework.

⚙️ run_today.py

The engine — pulls prices, scores, writes the 3 profiles into today.json.

🧪 backtest*.py

The honesty checks — luck bar, calibration, hold-returns, costs.

📄 today.json

The precomputed data contract the website reads.

🌐 index.html

The site — 4 sentences, filters, risk quiz, TH/EN, dark/light.

Run it yourself: pip install pandas numpy yfinance then python run_today.py (refresh picks) or python backtest.py (the honesty check). Full state = CONTINUE.md · full design = HANDOFF.md.
