⚡ The 60-second version
SETScout finds Thai stocks worth researching — and tells you the risk, honestly. It does not predict prices or tell you to buy. Every day it scores ~92 SET100 stocks on 5 simple factors, sorts them into Worth a look 🟢 / Wait 🟡 / Not now 🔴, and explains each in plain sentences with the risk in baht. It even admits when it can't beat the market — that honesty is the point.

🔍 The detailed version

What it is (and isn't). SETScout is an Explainable Investment Decision Support System for the Thai market. It answers “which stocks should I spend time researching today, and how risky are they?” — not “what will the price be” and not “buy this.” Discovery + risk + explanation, with the disclaimer everywhere.

The 5 factors (the “score”)

📈 Momentum

6-month return — is it going well?

🌱 Growth

12-month return — the longer trend.

⚖️ Value

Cheap vs its own 200-day average.

🛡️ Quality

Low volatility — calm, not wild.

❤️ Health

Small max-drawdown — survives bad markets.

Each factor is z-scored (put on a fair 0-centred scale) and sector-neutralized — a stock is judged against its own sector peers, so one calm sector (like banks) can't sweep the top. · ให้คะแนนเทียบกับหุ้นในกลุ่มเดียวกัน
The 3 engines (personalization)

Same 5 factors, three weightings — one per risk appetite. Your risk-quiz answer picks which list you see. This is a personalized recommender: user profile → matching model.

🛡️ Conservative

quality 40 · health 30 · value 20 · mom 5 · growth 5
Calm, lower-risk picks. Trails the market — by design.

⚖️ Balanced

quality 28 · value 24 · mom 20 · health 16 · growth 12
The middle default. Also defensive-leaning.

🚀 Aggressive

mom 40 · growth 30 · value 15 · quality 10 · health 5
Beats buy-and-hold at 1y/5y/10y — the momentum effect.
The honest findings (from real backtests)
Engine (buy picks, hold 12mo)returnvs buy&holdbeats random
🛡️ Conservative+1.3%−5.40%
⚖️ Balanced+1.9%−4.80%
🚀 Aggressive+9.0%+2.396%

Read it honestly: the defensive engines don't beat the market — that's expected (efficient markets) and it's the thesis, not a bug. The aggressive engine does show a real edge (the well-documented momentum premium) — and it survives trading costs (+126% net over 10y vs +86% buy&hold, even at 1% round-trip). Caveats we keep out loud: survivorship bias in old data, momentum can crash, one market/period.

The honesty rails (why judges trust it)
Luck bar — we compare against hundreds of random portfolios. Real skill beats most of them; luck doesn't.
Calibration — we checked if a high score predicts an up-move. It doesn't (flat ~47%). So the honest p_win is ~47% — we rank research-worthiness, not direction.
No look-ahead — every backtest scores using only past data, then measures the future.
Educational, not advice — soft wording, disclaimers everywhere, non-commercial.

Under the hood (for builders)

The engine runs ahead of time and writes a file; the website just reads it — so users never wait, and the site could be built before the engine even existed. Everything is Python + vanilla JS, no framework.

⚙️ run_today.py

The engine — pulls prices, scores, writes the 3 profiles into today.json.

🧪 backtest*.py

The honesty checks — luck bar, calibration, hold-returns, costs.

📄 today.json

The precomputed data contract the website reads.

🌐 index.html

The site — 4 sentences, filters, risk quiz, TH/EN, dark/light.

Run it yourself: pip install pandas numpy yfinance then python run_today.py (refresh picks) or python backtest.py (the honesty check). Full state = CONTINUE.md · full design = HANDOFF.md.
