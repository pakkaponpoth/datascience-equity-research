SETScout · System Map

An Explainable Investment Decision Support System for the Thai stock market — points you to SET stocks worth investigating, and explains why. It doesn't predict prices or trade.
① What the user actually sees — 4 plain sentences
1 · VERDICT

Looks good ✅ / Wait 🟡 / Not now 🔴

Honest enough to say "none today."

2 · RISK (in baht)

How much could hurt

"A normal-bad month ≈ −16%. Put at most ฿23 of every ฿100 here."
3 · BECAUSE

The reason, in one line

"Price is above its long average and hasn't just spiked."
4 · TRUST LABEL

How much to believe us

"Tested on 10 yrs. When we say 60%, it happened ~60%."
