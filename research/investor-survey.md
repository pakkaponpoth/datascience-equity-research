# SETScout — Retail-Investor Survey (primary data · Stream 2)

> Goal: (1) prove the problem is real, (2) learn what users trust & understand,
> (3) test whether our 4-sentence explanation actually works.
> Format: Google Forms, ~7 min. Target: 100–300 Thai retail investors.
> **This is about people, not stock prices** — behavioural/attitudinal data.

## Section A — Who you are (screening + segments)

1. Do you currently invest in Thai stocks (SET), or plan to within a year?
   ⬜ Yes, actively · ⬜ Yes, occasionally · ⬜ Planning to start · ⬜ No *(→ end)*
2. How long have you invested? ⬜ <1 yr · ⬜ 1–3 · ⬜ 3–5 · ⬜ 5+ · ⬜ not yet
3. Roughly how much do you invest in stocks? ⬜ <฿50k · ⬜ ฿50k–200k · ⬜ ฿200k–1M · ⬜ >฿1M · ⬜ prefer not to say
4. Age band: ⬜ <20 · ⬜ 20–29 · ⬜ 30–39 · ⬜ 40–49 · ⬜ 50+

## Section B — How you pick stocks today (the problem)

5. When you look for a new stock to buy, how do you find candidates? *(check all)*
   ⬜ friends/family · ⬜ social media / YouTube · ⬜ broker app (Streaming/Settrade) · ⬜ news · ⬜ my own screening · ⬜ analyst reports · ⬜ I don't really know where to start
6. *"I often don't know which stock to even start looking at."*
   ⬜ Strongly disagree — ⬜ — ⬜ — ⬜ — ⬜ Strongly agree *(1–5 Likert)*
7. *"Researching a stock before buying takes more time/skill than I have."* (1–5 Likert)
8. Biggest frustration in finding stocks? *(open text, optional)*

## Section C — Trust & sources

9. How much would you trust a **recommendation generated by an algorithm**? (1 = not at all … 5 = completely)
10. What would make you trust it MORE? *(check all)* ⬜ it explains *why* · ⬜ it shows the risk · ⬜ it shows its past accuracy · ⬜ it admits when unsure · ⬜ a human expert built it · ⬜ nothing would
11. *"A tool that shows the risk is more useful than one that predicts the price."* (1–5 Likert)

## Section D — Financial literacy (the validated "Big Three")

12. ฿100 in a savings account at 2%/yr — after 5 years you have:
    ⬜ more than ฿110 · ⬜ exactly ฿110 · ⬜ less than ฿110 · ⬜ don't know
13. If interest is 1%/yr and inflation is 2%/yr, in a year your money buys:
    ⬜ more · ⬜ the same · ⬜ **less** · ⬜ don't know
14. *"Buying one company's stock is usually safer than a stock mutual fund."*
    ⬜ True · ⬜ **False** · ⬜ don't know

## Section E — Risk attitude

15. A stock you own drops 20% in a month. You: ⬜ buy more · ⬜ hold · ⬜ sell some · ⬜ sell all · ⬜ panic
16. Preferred trade-off: ⬜ small steady gains, low risk · ⬜ balanced · ⬜ big gains, big risk

## Section F — 🔑 Comprehension test (does our explanation work?)

> Show this sample SETScout card, then ask:
> **Verdict:** 🟡 Wait · **Risk:** "a normal-bad month here ≈ −16%, so put at most ฿23 of every ฿100" · **Because:** "price is above its long average and hasn't just spiked" · **Trust:** "tested on 10 yrs; when we say 60% it happened ~60%."

17. In your own words, what is this card telling you to do? *(open text)*
18. *"I understood this without needing to look anything up."* (1–5 Likert)
19. Which sentence was **most** useful? ⬜ Verdict · ⬜ Risk · ⬜ Because · ⬜ Trust
20. Which was **confusing**, if any? ⬜ none · ⬜ Verdict · ⬜ Risk · ⬜ Because · ⬜ Trust

## Section G — Would you use it

21. *"I would use a free tool like this to find stocks to research."* (1–5 Likert)
22. Anything you'd want it to also do? *(open text, optional)*
